## GUIDE TO FILLING OUT FORMS AND AVOIDING ERRORS

Page to start with the access request to EU access . If you are not an Operator of a third country Installation(outside the EU or included in Annex III), your request will be rejected.

These points must be completed exactly as shown in the screen shot. If they are not, the request will be rejected

Identity: this field must display 'Request new identity' Type of Person: this field must display 'Legal Person' Legal person: this field must be completed with the Name of the company as it appears in the supporting document and in transliterated internationalized name.

Type of Actor: this field must display 'Third country Operator' Type of Identifier: this field must display "ORG\_ID"

Both boxes must always be checked for the request.

Company Address: the address of the company must be completed as it appears on the supporting document using English character/translation.

Company Email: It is recommended to use a corporate email address of the company making the request.

Company Website: this field is not mandatory, but it is a recommendation to facilitate the application.

Company Identifier: It is extremely important that the organization's identification number is entered exactly as it appears on the supporting document. Otherwise, the application will be rejected.

Company ISO-2 Country Code: the field refers to the country's nomenclature according to the ISO standard.

Support Document: The supporting document must be official documents from each of the countries where the installation is located:

- -Commercial registry or certificate of incorporation of the installation.
- Identification of the legal representative.
