## CBAM REGISTRATION GUIDE FOR NON-EU OPERATORS

One Pager Overview

## WHAT IS THIS FOR?

Non-EU operators can submit installations and emissions data via the CBAM O3CI Portal

## STEP 1:

Create EU Login Account

1. Go to https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/cas
2. Register  with  email  ( company  email  strongly  recommended ), create password, confirm via email link.
3. Set up 2-Factor Authentication (EU Login App, QR code, or FIDO2 key) - See page 12 of our guidance document for more information
4. Each user must register individually.


## STEP 2:

Submit Access Request (Onboarding Wizard)

1. Go to https://cbam.ec.europa.eu/o3cinstallation
- Log in using your EU Login credentials.
- Complete the onboarding form with:
- Company details and registration number/identifiers
- Company email and website
- - User role: Admin Operator or Simple Operator
- -Upload  a single  PDF  containing  (We  strongly  recommend submitting your documents in English, such as a courtesy translation, along with the original documents):
- * Company  registration certificate (include the  company registration  number/relevant  identifier,  ensuring  it  matches  the identifier provided in the onboarding form)
- * Proof of representation (e.g., power of attorney)
- * ID of the authorized representative

Example identifiers (not to be used in your request):

- IN123456ABCD (IN for the 2-letter country-code for India, followed by some letters and numbers)
- BR.XYZ-1234567890 (BR for the 2-letter country-code for Brazil, followed by a separator (dot), followed by other characters.

## We recommend using the following identifiers for the countries listed below:

- For China: Unified Social Credit (USC) number
- For India: Corporate Identification Number (CIN) or Limited Liability Partnership Identification Number (LLPIN)
- For the United Kingdom: Company Registration Number (CRN)
- For the United States: Central Index Key (CIK)
- For South Korea: Business registration number (TIN)
- For Turkey: Vergi Kimlik Numarasi
- For Ukraine: Registration number (EDRPOU)
- For others: Business or trade register number, tax identification number, or Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)

## STEP 3: Await Approval

- Review and submit access request.
- Monitor  email  address  linked  to  the  EU  Login  account  for confirmation and next steps from the Commission.
- Please note that the approval process may take several weeks
- Keep data accurate and up to date.
- EU Login Help: https://ecas.ec.europa.eu/cas/help.html
- Use only authorized EU Login accounts.
- CBAM Helpdesk: support@itsmtaxud.europa.eu
- (Mon-Fri, 07:00-21:00 CET) Include: error screenshots, exact URL, and time of issue
